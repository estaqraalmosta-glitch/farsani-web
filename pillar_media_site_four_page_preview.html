Plate 04 — Worked example

Find the pillar first.
Everything else is downstream.

Take a coffee shop. Three could open on the same street and all survive — because each is built on a different pillar.

Atmosphere

A vibe you can't get at home. Cultural references, a room that means something.

Quality

Specialty drinks. A range of beans. People who can tell you where it came from.

Reliability

Somewhere to sit for three hours. Wi-Fi that works. A price you don't think about.

S/01

Video

Moving pictures do the heavy lifting. They carry tone, pace and proof in a way a still frame can't.

  • Motion graphics
    Turning a process, an offer or a number into something watchable.
  • Storytelling
    Short films with a beginning, a turn and a reason to care.
  • Engaging short reels
    Cut for the feed — vertical, fast, built to survive the thumb.
  • Professional interviews
    Founders, staff and customers, lit and directed so they sound like themselves.

S/02

Posters & Pictures

Stills go where video can't — a wall, a window, a printed menu, a feed on mute.

  • High visual impact
    Work that holds up both offline and online, at any size.
  • Consistent identity
    Every piece reinforces the same brand, not a new one each week.
  • Ideas at a glance
    Complex propositions made legible in the two seconds you actually get.
  • Multi-channel proof
    Showing you can communicate anywhere signals a business worth trusting.
